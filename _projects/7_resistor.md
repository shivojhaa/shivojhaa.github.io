Devices were fabricated on a p-type Si wafer, measured after fabrication, and compared with simulation in Synopsys Sentaurus and with analytical calculations.

- Fabrication: PVD, PECVD, RCA processes and lithography
- Simulation: process flow and device physics in Sentaurus
- Result: a fabricated Si resistor with a sheet resistance of 366 Ω/□
