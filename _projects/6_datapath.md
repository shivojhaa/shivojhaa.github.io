A custom CMOS 8-bit datapath designed in Cadence Virtuoso on the AMIC5N process. I designed the 8×8 SRAM and the domino decoder, and I routed the components. The project received the Outstanding Project Award.

- SRAM timing: 1.11 ns write, 2.86 ns read
- 8-bit domino decoder: 300–900 ps
- Full datapath: under 10 ns
- Transistor density: 0.0112 transistors/µm²
